WHO WE ARE
LankaLux runs private, tailor-made journeys around Sri Lanka, from a team based in Sri Lanka. There are no group coaches and no fixed packages. Every trip is designed around the guest. Tagline: "Private journeys, exceptional care."

WHAT IS INCLUDED

WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED (unless the itinerary says so)

HOW MANY DAYS
Sri Lanka looks compact, but the distances are not. Don't promise four regions in five nights.
- 7 days: choose two regions, for example culture and coast, or hills and a park.
- 10 days: best for most first visits. Cultural Triangle, then Kandy and tea country, then a safari, then the south coast.
- 14 days: add a second park only if wildlife is the point; otherwise spend the extra nights doing less.

PLACES (typical pacing)
- Sigiriya: a fifth-century rock fortress. Climb in the cool morning. Two nights nearby is comfortable.
- Kandy: the lake town with the Temple of the Tooth. Shoulders and knees must be covered, and shoes come off. One night if moving on, two is kinder.
- Ella and the hill country: tea views. Two nights. Little Adam's Peak and Nine Arches Bridge are both popular; do one of them well.
- The hill-country train: slow and beautiful. Seats are booked in advance where possible and tickets cost extra. The chauffeur-guide drops guests at the station and meets them with the bags. For children or limited mobility, a tea-country drive can replace it.
- Yala: the best park for leopards. Two nights allows a morning and an evening safari.
- Udawalawe: reliable elephants, and often gentler than Yala for families.
- Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed, in any park. Don't schedule dawn safaris in two parks on back-to-back days.
- Galle: the UNESCO-listed Fort. Two nights is enough.
- Mirissa: beach time. At least two nights after an inland week, often three for families. Whale watching is seasonal and optional, never guaranteed, and the boat fee is separate.

WEATHER
Two monsoons affect different coasts. The south and west are generally drier in the northern-hemisphere winter, and the east coast is different later in the year. The hill country can be cool and misty at any time, so guests should pack a layer. Don't promise the weather.

FAMILIES
Put pace before highlights. With a private vehicle, an early return or a skipped viewpoint is normal. A van such as the Voxy gives room for child seats. Dawn safaris are optional for families. Never give medical or visa advice.
